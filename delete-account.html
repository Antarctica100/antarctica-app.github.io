Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Request account deletion

Sign in with the existing Antarctica account you want to delete. After authentication, review the account details and explicitly confirm your deletion request.

Account deletion is intended to remove your account and associated data through our deletion process, subject to applicable legal retention requirements and any limitations explained in our Privacy Policy.

1. Verify your account

Use the email address associated with your existing Antarctica account. This page does not ask for your password. The sign-in request is configured not to create a new account.

2. Confirm your account

Signed-in account:

Make sure this is the account you want to delete. Deletion is intended to be permanent and may remove your profile, friendships, messages, photos and other associated data. Some information may be retained where required or permitted by law.

Deletion request received

Your account-deletion request was accepted for processing.

The cleanup process may take time and may retry failed operations. This confirmation means the request was queued; it does not mean all account data has already been deleted.

Account requested for deletion:

What happens next?

Your request is submitted to Antarctica's account-deletion backend. The backend may process cleanup asynchronously and retry failed operations. An accepted request does not mean every item has already been deleted.

Some information may be retained where required or permitted by law. For details about information collected, processing, retention and deletion, read our Privacy Policy.

Need help?

Email antarctica.support@gmail.com if you cannot sign in or need help with a deletion request.
