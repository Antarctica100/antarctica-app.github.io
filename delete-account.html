Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Request deletion of your Antarctica account

You can request deletion of your Antarctica account and its associated data even if you no longer have the app installed. This page provides an external way to submit your request.

App: Antarctica
Developer: Individual developer
Contact: antarctica.support@gmail.com

Option 1: Request deletion by email

Send an account-deletion request from the email address associated with your Antarctica account, if you can access it. Include the following information in your request:

  • That you want your Antarctica account deleted.
  • The email address associated with the account, so we can identify it.
  • Whether you can still access the account, if relevant.

Do not include your password, authentication codes or other sign-in secrets. We may contact you if reasonable additional information is needed to verify the request.

Start deletion request by email

This button opens your email application with a draft request. You must send the email for your request to reach us. If it does not open, email antarctica.support@gmail.com directly.

Option 2: Request deletion inside the app

If you can still sign in, use the account-deletion option in Antarctica's Settings screen and follow the confirmation steps. You do not need to submit a second request if your in-app request has already been accepted.

What data is affected?

A completed account-deletion request is intended to remove the account and associated personal data covered by the deletion process. This may include your profile and account information, task-related server data, social connections, and associated messages or media where applicable to the system's deletion rules.

Some data may need to be retained where required or permitted by law, or for legitimate security, fraud-prevention or compliance purposes. Temporary processing records may also be used to complete a deletion request.

For further information, read the Antarctica Privacy Policy.

How long does deletion take?

Deletion requests may be processed asynchronously, so the process may not finish immediately. We will aim to handle valid requests within a reasonable period and in accordance with applicable law.

For a status enquiry, contact antarctica.support@gmail.com and refer to your account-deletion request.

Contact and related policies

Questions about account deletion can be sent to antarctica.support@gmail.com.
