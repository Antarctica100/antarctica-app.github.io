Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Request account deletion

Sign in with the account you want to delete. After authentication, you can review and explicitly confirm your deletion request.

Account deletion is intended to remove your account and associated data according to our deletion process and applicable legal retention requirements.

1. Verify your account

The magic link will return you to this page. Use the email address associated with your existing Antarctica account. This page does not ask for your password.

2. Confirm your account

Signed-in account:

Make sure this is the account you want to delete. This action is intended to be permanent and may remove your profile, friendships, messages, photos and other associated data.

What happens next?

Your request is submitted to Antarctica's account-deletion backend. The backend may process cleanup asynchronously and retry failed operations. An accepted request does not mean every item has already been deleted.

Some information may be retained where required or permitted by law. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Need help?

Email antarctica.support@gmail.com if you cannot sign in or need help with a deletion request.
