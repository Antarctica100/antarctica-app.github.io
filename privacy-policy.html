Antarctica — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

1. Who We Are

Antarctica (“the App”) helps users manage tasks, develop routines, improve focus, manage app usage, and interact with friends through available social features. The App is operated by an individual developer (“we”, “us”, or “our”).

For privacy questions or requests, contact antarctica.support@gmail.com.

2. Information We Process

The information processed depends on the features you use.

2.1 Account and Profile Information

We may process your email address, authentication identifiers, public user identifier, profile information, account status, and settings needed to authenticate users and maintain account security. If you sign in using Google, Google processes authentication information under its own privacy policy. We do not ask for your Google password.

2.2 Age-Related Information

The App uses age-related information to determine eligibility and apply age-based restrictions and adult-approval requirements. The App is designed to retain the processed age or minor/adult status needed for these features rather than the user's raw date of birth.

2.3 Tasks and Device Settings

Depending on your use, we may process tasks, timetables, schedules, alarms, reminders, focus modes, app usage limits, and settings or permissions needed for notifications, widgets, and app-blocking features. Some information may be stored locally on your device rather than on our servers.

2.4 Friends and Social Features

We may process friend requests and friendship status, blocked-user information, group membership and invitations, social settings, consent or adult-approval status, and information needed to enforce eligibility and safety controls.

2.5 Messages and Photos

Individual and group chats may process message content, account identifiers, timestamps, delivery information, and associated photos. Chat photos may be stored in private cloud storage, with access controlled by backend permissions and social-access rules.

Some chat features are designed to deliver messages when recipients become available and remove server-side message data after delivery. Retention can vary by data type and delivery or cleanup status.

2.6 Analytics, Diagnostics, and Notifications

We may use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand app usage, engagement, and feature reliability, and Firebase Crashlytics or similar diagnostic tools to identify crashes and technical problems, if these services are enabled in a release.

Depending on configuration, these services may process app events, device or app-instance identifiers, app version, device information, and diagnostic or crash details. Analytics and diagnostics configuration may change as the App is developed.

The App may also process device notification tokens and related technical information to deliver notifications.

2.7 Reports and Safety Information

When users block or report others, we may process relevant account identifiers, report details, and moderation or safety records to investigate abuse and enforce App rules.

3. How We Use Information

We process information to create and authenticate accounts; provide tasks, schedules, alarms, reminders, focus tools, and app-blocking features; enable friendships, groups, and chat delivery; apply age restrictions and adult approvals; deliver notifications; manage blocks and reports; understand app performance and usage where analytics is enabled; diagnose technical problems; process account deletion; maintain security; and meet applicable legal obligations.

4. Service Providers

Antarctica uses Supabase for backend functionality, including authentication, database services, and chat-photo storage. Depending on production configuration, the App may also use Google authentication, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics, and other providers needed to operate the App.

These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services and under their applicable terms and privacy policies.

5. How Information May Be Shared

We do not sell users' personal information as a business practice. Information may be shared:

  • With other users: as needed for profiles, friendships, groups, messages, and social features.
  • With service providers: for authentication, hosting, storage, notifications, analytics, diagnostics, and other enabled services.
  • For safety and legal reasons: where necessary to address abuse, protect users, respond to lawful requests, or comply with applicable law.
  • For a business transfer: if the App or its assets are transferred, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Information shared with other users may be copied, screenshotted, or saved by recipients. Deleting it from the App cannot guarantee removal of copies already made by others.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, subject to applicable law. Account information is retained while needed to operate the account and process deletion. Tasks and settings are retained as needed to provide features. Chat information may be removed after delivery or through cleanup processes. Reports and safety records may be retained as needed to investigate abuse or comply with law.

Backups and technical records may remain for a limited period after deletion, depending on system configuration. When information is no longer required, we intend to delete it or otherwise handle it in accordance with applicable law.

7. Account Deletion

Users can request account deletion through the relevant option in the App, where available. Deletion may be processed asynchronously, and removing account records, chat photos, and other backend data may require additional cleanup.

We intend to delete associated personal information unless retention is necessary or permitted by law. Signing out does not delete an account. Use the account-deletion feature to request deletion.

8. Security

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect information, including authentication, access controls, and backend security rules. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Protect your device and account access, and contact us if you suspect unauthorized access.

9. Age and Adult Approval

Antarctica applies age-related restrictions and may require adult approval before certain social features can be used. Users should provide accurate age-related information. We will apply applicable legal requirements concerning children, consent, and personal information.

10. Your Privacy Requests

Subject to applicable law, users may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information, or to ask questions about its processing. We may verify identity before acting on a request and will handle requests in accordance with applicable law.

Contact: antarctica.support@gmail.com

11. International Processing

Service providers may process or store information outside the user's state or country, depending on their infrastructure and configuration. Where applicable, transfers and processing will be subject to relevant legal requirements.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy when the App, its data practices, or applicable legal requirements change. We will update the “Last updated” date when we revise the Policy and provide additional notice where required.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact antarctica.support@gmail.com.
