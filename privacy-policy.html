Antarctica — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Antarctica ("Antarctica", "the App", "we", "us", or "our") may collect, use, store, disclose, and delete information when you use the App, its website, and related services. It covers features available in the current version and describes certain optional or future integrations where indicated.

1. Who We Are and How to Contact Us

Antarctica is operated by an individual developer. The App is designed to help users organize tasks and routines, improve focus, manage app usage, track progress, set alarms and reminders, and use available friendship, group, and messaging features.

For privacy questions, data requests, or concerns, contact: antarctica.support@gmail.com.

The App's current operator is an individual developer, not a company identified by this Policy as a separate legal entity.

2. Information We Process

The information processed depends on your account, device, permissions, settings, and the features you use. Some information is sent to our backend; other information may remain on your device. Not every category described below is necessarily collected in every release.

2.1 Account, Authentication, and Profile Information

We may process your email address, authentication-provider identifiers, internal user identifier, public user identifier, profile name, avatar, profile settings, account status, and information needed to authenticate you and maintain account security.

You may be able to sign in using email-based authentication or Google sign-in through the configured authentication provider. When Google sign-in is used, Google processes information under its own applicable privacy terms. Antarctica does not ask you to provide your Google password directly to the App.

Profile details and public identifiers may be visible to other users where required for friendship, group, leaderboard, or other social features. The information displayed depends on the feature and its access controls.

2.2 Age, Date of Birth, and Adult Approval

The App uses age-related information to determine eligibility, apply age-based restrictions, and decide whether adult approval is required before certain social features can be enabled. The signup flow may ask for a date of birth or other age information for this purpose.

The intended backend design stores the processed age category or minor/adult status needed to apply these restrictions rather than retaining the raw date of birth in the social-controls record. Information entered during signup may also be temporarily processed by the device or authentication flow. This statement does not mean that no raw date-of-birth value can exist in any device cache, log, or other system; actual retention depends on the relevant implementation.

Where adult approval is required, we may process a parent's or guardian's email address, approval-request status, approval timestamps, verification-code data in protected form, expiration information, and related records needed to administer the approval process. Verification codes are intended for limited use and may expire or become invalid after repeated unsuccessful attempts.

Please do not submit another person's personal information unless you are authorized to provide it for the relevant purpose.

2.3 Tasks, Timetables, Goals, and Progress

Depending on your use, we may process task names and descriptions, scheduled start and end times, recurring schedules, reminders, task classifications, focus settings, streaks, levels, progress, completion records, and other information you enter or generate through the App.

This information is used to provide task planning, routines, timetable management, reminders, progress tracking, and related features. Some settings and records may be stored locally; others may be synchronized with backend services where the feature supports synchronization.

2.4 Device Permissions and App-Usage Controls

Features such as alarms, notifications, app blocking, and reminders may require Android permissions or access to particular device capabilities. Depending on your settings and the Android version, the App may use device app information, package identifiers, permission status, app-usage information made available to it, alarm scheduling, and local preferences to apply the limits and rules you configure.

App-usage or installed-app information may be processed on your device to support blocking and time-limit features. This Policy does not mean that a complete history of every app you use is necessarily uploaded to Antarctica's servers.

Android permissions and device restrictions may affect whether alarms, notifications, or app blocking work as expected. You can manage available permissions in your device settings. Revoking a permission may disable or limit the related feature.

2.5 Camera, Photos, Files, and Image Processing

Where you use a photo-related task, profile image, chat attachment, or other media feature, the App may access images or files you choose or capture, subject to the permissions and selection mechanisms available on your device.

If image labeling or similar on-device or provider-assisted processing is enabled, image data may be analyzed to support the requested feature. The precise processing location depends on the enabled implementation. We do not represent that all image analysis is exclusively on-device unless the relevant release is configured and verified to work that way.

Photos or files submitted for a task or social feature may be stored locally or uploaded to a backend service when required for that feature. Do not submit sensitive images or files unless you understand how the relevant feature uses them.

2.6 Friends, Groups, and Social Controls

If you use social features, we may process friend requests, friendship status, group names and descriptions, group membership, invitations, group warnings, blocked-user relationships, social preferences, consent records, adult-approval status, and related timestamps or identifiers.

This information supports social functionality, eligibility checks, group management, blocking, and safety controls. Some profile and relationship information must be shared with the relevant users for social features to work.

2.7 Chat Messages, Attachments, and Delivery Information

Individual and group messaging may process message text, links, photos, videos, other supported files, sender and recipient identifiers, timestamps, message type, delivery or acknowledgement status, and information needed to manage message visibility and cleanup.

Chat attachments may be held in private cloud storage, with access intended to be restricted by backend authorization and social-access rules. Authorized recipients may be able to view or download attachments while access is available.

Certain chat features are designed to remove server-side message records or media after the required delivery or acknowledgement conditions are met. Cleanup may be asynchronous and may be retried after a failure. This does not guarantee immediate deletion in every case, and retention can vary by message status, attachment type, technical failure, backup, or legal requirement.

Messages or attachments saved, copied, screenshotted, or downloaded by recipients may remain outside Antarctica's control. Deleting a server-side copy cannot guarantee deletion of copies already held by another user.

2.8 Notifications and Background Processing

If push notifications are enabled, the App or its service providers may process device notification tokens, account identifiers, notification preferences, and delivery status to send relevant notifications. Local alarms and reminders may be scheduled on your device independently of push notifications.

Background jobs may process pending notifications, age-related transitions, chat cleanup, account-deletion jobs, or other maintenance tasks needed for the relevant features. Such jobs may retain operational status and timestamps so that failed work can be retried or investigated.

2.9 Reports, Blocking, and Safety Records

When you block or report another user, we may process the relevant user identifiers, report category, details you submit, timestamps, moderation outcomes, block history, and other records needed to review abuse, enforce rules, and protect users.

Safety records may be retained separately from ordinary profile or chat content where reasonably necessary to prevent abuse, enforce restrictions, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

2.10 Speech, Text-to-Speech, and Language Features

If voice input, speech recognition, text-to-speech, translation, or related accessibility features are enabled and used, the App may process the text or audio necessary to provide the requested function. Depending on the specific implementation, processing may occur on-device or through an operating-system or third-party service. The App's use of a speech-related library alone does not establish that audio is uploaded to Antarctica's backend.

2.11 Analytics, Crash Reports, and Diagnostics

If analytics or diagnostic services are included and enabled in a release, those services may process app events, app version, device or app-instance identifiers, device information, crash traces, performance information, and technical logs. These data may help us understand feature usage, reliability, and technical problems.

Firebase Cloud Messaging is part of the App's intended notification integrations. Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or other analytics and diagnostic services may be used only if configured and enabled in the relevant release. Their presence in development dependencies does not by itself mean they are actively collecting information.

3. How We Use Information

Depending on the feature, we use information to:

  • Create, authenticate, secure, and maintain accounts.
  • Display and maintain profiles and user identifiers.
  • Provide tasks, timetables, routines, alarms, reminders, and progress features.
  • Apply device-based usage limits, focus settings, and app-blocking rules.
  • Support photo tasks, media attachments, and relevant image-processing features.
  • Enable friendships, groups, invitations, messages, and attachment delivery.
  • Apply age restrictions and manage required adult approval.
  • Send notifications and process necessary background work.
  • Investigate reports, enforce blocking and safety controls, and prevent abuse.
  • Diagnose failures, maintain reliability, and improve features where appropriate.
  • Process data-access, correction, and account-deletion requests.
  • Comply with applicable laws and protect the rights and security of users and the service.

4. Legal Bases and Permission Choices

Where applicable law requires a legal basis for processing, the relevant basis may include providing a feature you request, maintaining account security, pursuing legitimate interests such as preventing abuse and maintaining the service, obtaining consent where required, or complying with legal obligations.

Some processing depends on Android permissions or choices you make in the App. You can decline optional permissions or disable certain features, although features that depend on those permissions may not work. Withdrawing a device permission does not automatically delete information already stored on a server.

The legal basis applicable to a particular activity depends on the user's location, the feature, and the circumstances of processing. Nothing in this section removes rights provided by applicable law.

5. Service Providers and Technology

The App uses Supabase for backend functionality, including authentication, database operations, server-side functions, and private chat-photo storage. The App's configured authentication flow may use Google sign-in.

Depending on the release and enabled configuration, other providers or technologies may include:

  • Brevo: email delivery for features such as parent or guardian approval.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: push-notification delivery where enabled.
  • Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics: analytics and diagnostic processing only if enabled.
  • Android and device services: local alarms, notifications, app-usage controls, camera access, file selection, and speech-related features.

Other providers may be added if needed to operate a feature. Providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices and, where applicable, our service arrangements with them. The exact information each provider receives depends on the integration actually enabled.

We do not claim that every provider listed above is active in every release. We intend to update this Policy if material data practices change.

6. How Information May Be Shared

We do not sell personal information as a business practice. We may share or make information available in the following circumstances:

  • With other users: profile details, public identifiers, friendship and group information, messages, and media to the extent required by the social feature and its access rules.
  • With service providers: information needed for authentication, hosting, database operations, email delivery, notifications, storage, diagnostics, or other enabled functions.
  • For safety and legal reasons: where reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, protect users, enforce rules, respond to lawful requests, or comply with applicable law.
  • For a business or asset transfer: if the App or related assets are transferred, subject to applicable legal requirements and appropriate handling of personal information.
  • At your direction: when you choose to share content or use a feature that makes information available to other users.

We do not authorize service providers to use information received from us for purposes unrelated to their services where restricted by our applicable arrangements or law.

Information made available to other users may be copied, screenshotted, forwarded, or saved by them. We cannot guarantee that copies outside our systems will be removed when you delete content or an account.

7. Data Retention

We intend to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, taking account of the feature, security needs, applicable law, and legitimate dispute-resolution requirements.

  • Account and profile data: generally retained while needed to operate the account, subject to account deletion and applicable retention requirements.
  • Tasks, settings, and progress: retained as needed to provide the features, synchronize supported data, and maintain the user's requested experience.
  • Chat messages and media: certain server-side data is intended to be cleaned up after delivery or acknowledgement conditions are met. Actual cleanup may be delayed by retries or technical failures.
  • Adult-approval records: request, status, and verification information may be retained as needed to administer approval, enforce eligibility, and protect the process. Verification codes and tokens are intended to expire and be cleared when no longer needed.
  • Reports and safety records: may be retained for an appropriate period to address abuse, repeated violations, security, or legal obligations.
  • Deletion and technical records: job status, retry information, security logs, and limited operational records may remain temporarily to complete or verify deletion and maintain service security.

Backup systems, provider records, and technical logs may retain information for a limited period after it is deleted from active systems. Retention periods depend on the relevant configuration and provider. We do not promise immediate removal from every backup or every recipient's device.

8. Account Deletion and Clearing App Data

8.1 Account Deletion

Where available, the App provides a way to request account deletion. The request may be accepted first and completed asynchronously. Backend processing may need to remove account records, related database information, stored media, and the authentication account. Failed cleanup may be retried.

Acceptance of a deletion request does not necessarily mean every item has already been deleted. We intend to complete deletion of associated personal information, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements. Information that must be retained may be restricted from ordinary use for the period required.

To request account deletion, use the in-App account-deletion option where available or visit: Antarctica Account Deletion.

8.2 Clearing App Data

The App may also provide a separate option to clear or reset app data. This is not the same as deleting your account. Depending on the feature, clearing app data may remove profile-related app data, friends, groups, progress, tasks, timetables, or other app records while leaving the authenticated account and certain social-consent or approval records intact. The exact effect is described in the relevant confirmation screen.

8.3 Signing Out

Signing out does not, by itself, delete your account or submit an account-deletion request.

9. Security

We use technical measures intended to protect information, including authentication, access controls, backend authorization rules, restrictions on sensitive database operations, and private storage controls where configured. We seek to limit access to information according to the needs of each feature.

No application, device, transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. You should protect your device, secure access to your email and sign-in accounts, and contact us if you suspect unauthorized access or a privacy or security issue.

10. Children, Age Restrictions, and Adult Approval

Antarctica applies age-related eligibility restrictions and may disable certain social features until required adult approval is completed. The App is intended to enforce these restrictions through its age and social-control mechanisms.

Users should provide accurate age-related information and must not misrepresent their age to bypass restrictions. Where adult approval is required, the App may process the parent's or guardian's email address and approval status to request and record that approval.

We intend to handle children's information in accordance with applicable legal requirements. The precise rules may differ by country or region. Parents or guardians who have questions about information associated with a child may contact us using the address below.

11. Your Privacy Rights and Requests

Depending on applicable law and your circumstances, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or information about the processing of your personal information. You may also have rights to withdraw consent or raise a complaint with a competent authority where the law provides these rights.

Contact antarctica.support@gmail.com to make a privacy request. We may need to verify your identity or authority before responding, particularly when a request concerns a child's information. We will handle requests within the periods and subject to the exceptions provided by applicable law.

If you are a parent or guardian making a request on behalf of a child, please explain your relationship and the nature of your request. Do not email passwords, authentication tokens, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

12. International Processing

Our service providers may process or store information in countries other than the country where you live. The locations depend on provider infrastructure and configuration. Where required, transfers and processing will be subject to applicable legal safeguards.

13. Third-Party Services and Links

The App or its website may link to third-party services, including sign-in providers or service-provider privacy notices. Those services operate under their own terms and policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third parties, and you should review their policies before using their services.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Policy when the App's features, integrations, data practices, or legal requirements change. The date at the top indicates the date of the latest revision. Where required, we will provide additional notice or seek consent for material changes.

15. Contact

For privacy questions, concerns, or requests, contact: antarctica.support@gmail.com.

Operator: Individual developer of Antarctica.

This Policy describes intended and possible data practices based on the App's features and integrations. Optional integrations may not be active in every release. The Policy does not guarantee that every technical operation always succeeds or that deletion is instantaneous.
