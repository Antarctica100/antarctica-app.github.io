Privacy Policy

Antarctica · Published by CreativeMind

Effective date: 27 October 2026 · Last updated: 27 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CreativeMind (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use the Antarctica mobile application (“Antarctica” or the “App”). Please read it before using the App. This policy should be read together with any clear, in-app permission prompts and notices shown when you use a feature.

1. Who is responsible and how to contact us

Antarctica is published by CreativeMind. For privacy questions, requests, or complaints, contact antarctica.support@gmail.com.

2. Information we handle

The information handled depends on the features you use, the permissions you grant, and whether you create an account.

  • Account and authentication information: email address, authentication identifiers, sign-in provider information, and account/profile details such as display name or avatar that you provide or choose to use. If you use Google sign-in, Google processes sign-in information under its own privacy terms and provides authentication information to the App/service.
  • Age and parent/guardian consent: during signup, the App uses the age information you enter to determine whether the adult or minor flow applies. The App is designed not to send or store your full date of birth in the Antarctica database. The service may store an age category, consent-request status, parent/guardian email address, timestamps, an approval-token hash, an approval-code hash and expiry/attempt information needed to process consent securely. The raw approval code is not intended to be stored in the database.
  • Productivity information: tasks, schedules, timetable entries, alarm/reminder settings, focus or mode settings, app-blocking choices, and related progress or completion information you create in the App.
  • Friends, groups and communications: friendship or group membership information, messages, links, photos, videos, files, delivery/receipt status, message-expiry information, blocks, invitations, reports and related safety records, where you use these features.
  • Device and app information needed for features: device notification token(s), app/permission state, and information needed to identify apps you choose to manage with the app-blocking feature. Android may require permission to view installed apps or usage-related information for this feature. The App uses this information to provide the feature you request; it is not intended to be used to sell a profile of your app usage.
  • Permissions and locally processed information: depending on the feature, you may grant notification, alarm/scheduling, camera, photo/media/file access, microphone, speech recognition, or other Android permissions. These are used to provide the relevant feature, such as alarms, reminders, photo tasks, voice input, or sending media. Some settings and operational data may be stored locally on your device.
  • Support and safety information: bug reports, messages you send to support, user reports, and information needed to investigate misuse or protect the service.
  • Technical and operational information: information generated by the backend or service providers to authenticate requests, deliver notifications, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, and maintain security. Service-provider logs and diagnostics may also be generated.

Do not send information you do not want other users to see through social features. Do not include passwords, payment-card details, government identifiers, or other highly sensitive information in chat or bug reports.

3. How we use information

  • create and secure accounts, authenticate users, and maintain profiles;
  • provide tasks, schedules, alarms, reminders, focus modes, app-blocking and progress features;
  • operate friend, group, messaging, media-sharing, notification and safety features;
  • request, record and verify parent/guardian consent for minor accounts;
  • respond to support requests and bug reports, diagnose errors and improve reliability;
  • detect spam, abuse, fraud, policy violations or attempts to evade a valid enforcement action;
  • process account deletion and meet applicable legal obligations; and
  • protect the rights, safety and security of users and the service.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use a child’s date of birth as a stored profile field in the Antarctica database under the intended signup design described above.

4. When information is shared

We share information only as needed to operate the App, carry out a user-requested action, protect the service, or comply with law. Depending on the feature, recipients may include:

  • Supabase: backend database, authentication, storage and server functions used to operate accounts and app features.
  • Google: Google sign-in and related authentication services if you choose that sign-in method.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google): delivery of push notifications to devices.
  • Brevo: delivery of parent/guardian consent-request emails and approval-code emails.
  • Other users: friends or group members may see content and profile details you send or make available through social features. Only send content you are comfortable sharing with the intended recipients.
  • Authorities or other parties: where required by law, legal process, or necessary to protect users, rights, safety, or the service.

Service providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices as well as any applicable arrangements with us. Their systems may process information in countries other than your own.

5. Chat messages and shared media

Antarctica’s chat features are designed to support temporary messages and media, including cleanup after receipt or after the applicable expiry period. Actual deletion timing may depend on message type, delivery state, backend cleanup, storage processing and temporary technical failures. A recipient may see, copy, screenshot, download, or otherwise save content before it expires or is deleted. We cannot remove copies independently saved by another person or content that has already been exported outside the App.

We may retain limited delivery, expiry, report, moderation or security information for as long as needed to operate the feature, resolve failures, investigate abuse or enforce safety rules.

6. Children and parent/guardian consent

Antarctica is not intended for children under 13, and the signup process is designed to reject users under 13. Users aged 13–17 must complete the parent/guardian consent process before their account is created or enabled through the minor signup flow. Adults use the normal signup flow.

The consent process may use the child’s email address, parent/guardian email address, request status, secure token/code hashes, expiry times, approval/denial timestamps and limited attempt-control information. The parent/guardian receives an email to approve or deny the request; when approved, a one-time code is sent to the parent/guardian email address for the child to enter. The App should not be used to bypass age restrictions or parental consent.

A parent/guardian can contact us at antarctica.support@gmail.com with questions or requests relating to a minor’s information or account. We may need to verify the requester’s authority before acting.

7. Permissions and your choices

Android permission prompts explain access requested by the App. You can manage permissions through Android settings. If you deny or revoke a permission, the related feature may not work as intended. For example, alarms and reminders may require notification or exact-alarm-related access depending on your Android version and device settings; app blocking may require the relevant access to identify or manage selected apps; media, camera, microphone and speech features require their corresponding permissions.

Notifications can be disabled in Android settings. You can also stop using social features, avoid sharing content, and contact us about account deletion or privacy questions.

8. Security

We use technical and organisational measures intended to protect information, including authenticated access, server-side access controls, and secure transport where supported. No online service, device, network, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your device secure and do not share account credentials or one-time approval codes.

9. Data retention and deletion

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing the service, handling consent, resolving support issues, enforcing safety rules, meeting legal obligations, and maintaining security. Retention depends on the type of information and the reason it is needed.

  • Active account data: account/profile details, tasks, schedules, settings, progress and social-feature data are retained while needed to provide the account and features, unless you delete them earlier where the App supports it.
  • Parent/guardian consent records: consent requests, status, timestamps, email addresses, token/code hashes, expiry data and attempt-limit records are retained while needed to complete or evidence the consent process, protect it from abuse, and resolve disputes or legal obligations. Unneeded or expired requests are subject to cleanup.
  • Chat messages and media: temporary messages and media are subject to the feature’s receipt/expiry cleanup process. Deletion may not be instantaneous and can be delayed by delivery state, retries, outages or technical processing. Copies saved by recipients are outside our control.
  • Account deletion requests: deletion is processed through a backend job. The job record may remain temporarily while deletion is pending, being retried or being verified. The system may retry failed cleanup steps. This operational record is used to complete deletion and is not intended to preserve the deleted account for continued use.
  • Security, abuse and ban-prevention records: we may retain a limited record, such as a protected email fingerprint, enforcement reason, status, creation/update time, expiry or review information, where reasonably necessary to enforce a permanent or time-limited safety ban, prevent repeated abuse or evasion, or comply with law. Such records are kept separately from the deleted user account and limited to that purpose.
  • Technical logs, backups and provider records: limited copies may persist for a period in provider logs, backup systems or security records until the relevant retention or overwrite process completes. They are not intended to be used to restore a deleted account for ordinary use.
  • Support and bug reports: retained for as long as needed to respond, investigate, prevent repeated abuse and maintain a record of the issue, then deleted or de-identified when no longer needed, subject to legal or security needs.

We do not promise that every copy is erased at the exact moment you submit a deletion request. We aim to process requests promptly; some backend cleanup is asynchronous and may require retries. If a deletion appears stuck, contact antarctica.support@gmail.com.

How to request deletion: use the account-deletion option inside Antarctica or the external account-deletion webpage linked from our official GitHub Pages site and Google Play listing. Follow the instructions on that page. We will use the information necessary to locate and verify the account and process the request. Deleting the account does not necessarily delete copies another user has saved, limited security/ban-prevention records, records we must keep by law, or copies awaiting routine backup/log expiry.

10. International processing

Our service providers may process or store information in locations outside your state or country. Where required, we take steps intended to ensure appropriate safeguards for such processing.

Email antarctica.support@gmail.com. Please do not send passwords or approval codes. We may ask for reasonable information to verify identity or parental authority before responding. Rights and procedures may also be available under applicable law.
